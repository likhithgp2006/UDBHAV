ABOUT UDBHAV 2026

Organized by the Department of Commerce through the Business Club at St. Claret College (Autonomous), Bengaluru.

The Flagship Commerce Arena

UDBHAV is the flagship intra-departmental fest organized by the Department of Commerce through the Business Club. It serves as an empowering platform for students to apply classroom knowledge to practical business scenarios.

The fest brings together students from all years to compete in business-oriented events that foster healthy competition, innovation, and professional excellence. Through UDBHAV, students gain exposure to real-world commerce, finance, management, and marketing concepts beyond academics.

BIZTOPIA — The City of Commerce

Biztopia is a fictional city where every street, district, and institution is driven by commerce. It represents the dynamic ecosystem of business, where leadership, finance, innovation, entrepreneurship, marketing, and global trade come together.

Four distinct teams represent different strengths: Aura drives wealth, Sync connects markets, Cresta fuels innovation, and Zephora elevates standards.

7 CORE FEST OBJECTIVES

1. Leadership & Governance

Develop core managerial competencies and ethical corporate decision making under pressure.

2. Entrepreneurial Thinking

Encourage innovative business models, pitch feasibility, and investor defense.

3. Teamwork & Collaboration

Promote cross-functional teamwork, strategic delegation, and unified execution.

4. Oratory & Public Speaking

Enhance executive communication, persuasion skills, and articulate presentations.

5. Financial & Analytics

Strengthen financial reporting, mock stock risk analysis, and corporate accounting.

6. Creative Innovation

Foster creative marketing campaigns, digital transformation, and out-of-the-box thinking.

7. Practical Learning Bridge

Bridge theoretical commerce learning with live, high-impact corporate application.

EXPECTED OUTCOME

Ethical, confident, and industry-ready future business leaders.
